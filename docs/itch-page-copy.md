## Full description (page body)

**SHIFT** is a tiny puzzle-platformer built for **Game Off 2026** (theme: SHIFT).

The world exists in two states. In State A, cyan blocks are solid and orange blocks are ghosts. Press **SHIFT** and everything inverts: solid becomes ghost, ghost becomes solid, the spikes change sides, and the whole palette flips with it. You keep your position and momentum — the world changes around you.

Every level fits on one screen, and every level teaches one idea: flip to build a bridge, flip to delete a wall, flip mid-jump to catch a platform that doesn't exist yet, flip while falling through a floor, chain two flips across a void. Touch the wrong state's spikes and you're back at the start.

### Features

- 8 hand-designed one-screen levels, each built around a single flip idea
- Instant world-flip with palette swap, screen flash, and ghost previews of the other state
- Fair by design: a flip that would materialize a block inside you is denied — no crushing, no soft-locks
- Forgiving platforming: coyote time and jump buffering
- No downloads — plays right in the browser

### Controls

- **Move:** A / D or arrow keys
- **Jump:** Space / W / Up
- **Flip the world:** SHIFT (either one)
- **Restart level:** R
- **Title screen:** Esc
